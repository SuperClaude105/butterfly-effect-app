How it works

Three steps to your book.

From idea to finished novel in minutes. No writing required.

01
Choose your world

Pick from 20+ genres — romance, fantasy, thriller, sci-fi, horror, and more. Write a premise or leave it blank and we'll invent one.

02
Shape the story

Name your characters, choose themes, set chapter length, and select what to keep out. Content warnings, series arcs, sequels — the AI follows your direction exactly.

03
Read and decide

Each chapter ends with a branching decision that rewrites what comes next. Your choices steer the plot in real time — no two reads are ever the same. Your finished book lives in your library forever.
