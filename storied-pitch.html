How it works

Six questions. One book.

You don't need to be a writer. You just need to know them.

01
Tell us about the two people

Names, ages, where they're from, what makes them who they are. Three personality traits each. Fill in as much or as little as you know.

02
Add the animals in their life

Pets, horses, any animal that's part of their story. The AI will weave them in naturally wherever they belong.

03
Build their timeline

Key events in chronological order — milestones, challenges, funny moments, romantic moments. The more specific, the more accurate the story.

Milestones Challenges Funny moments Romantic moments
04
Add the texture

Their restaurant. Their song. Inside jokes. The funniest memory they still laugh about. This is where the story stops being generic and starts being unmistakably theirs.

05
Write anything else

A free-text box with no rules. Write whatever you want woven in — the AI interprets it. This is where people put the things that don't fit anywhere else.

06
Choose the style

Relationship type, tone, point of view, narrative style. Heartwarming or bittersweet. Novel, memoir, or fairy tale. It's their story — you decide how it sounds.

Questions

Everything you need to know.

Who is Storied for?
Anyone who knows two people with a story worth telling. Spouses, best friends, a parent and child, siblings — even two business partners who built something together. If you can answer the six questions, we can write their book.
How long is a Storied book?
A standard Storied book is 5 chapters — typically 8,000–12,000 words, similar in length to a long short story. Each chapter focuses on a different period or theme of their relationship. You can request more chapters if their story needs more room.
Will it really sound like them?
The more detail you give, the more accurate it becomes. The form asks for personality traits, nicknames, specific memories, inside jokes, and their unique texture as a couple or pair. When those details are rich, the result is unmistakably theirs — not a generic love story.
Can the people in the story read it?
Yes — once the story is created, it's saved to your shelf. You can read it in the web reader chapter by chapter, or download it as a PDF to print or share. It's yours to give however you want.
Does it go into the public library?
No — Storied stories are private by default and never shared publicly unless you choose to make them public. The people in the story are real, so privacy is the default.
How do I start?
Create an account, then click "Your Story" in the navigation to open the Storied form. The six-step questionnaire saves as you go, so you can take your time filling it in.
